How to use this demo

Software for one made-up home-care agency in Birmingham. Take any of the four roles. You get your own private copy, so nothing you do reaches anyone else, and everything resets when the service restarts.

Short on time?

These three show the most in five minutes.

  1. Write a visit note with no signal, as a carer.
  2. Decide each finding in a flagged note, as the safeguarding lead.
  3. Confirm a new client's referral, as the coordinator.

Carer

Best on a phone. On a computer the same screens spread wider.

See your day and route

  1. On the sign-in page, choose a number under Your carer number.
  2. Select Sign in as this carer.
  3. Read To write up first, then your next visit and when to leave.
  4. Under Your route, select Zoom in, Zoom out or Whole route.
  5. Select a day in the week to see that day's visits.

Write a visit note, even with no signal

  1. After your day opens, wait a few seconds so the phone can save it.
  2. Turn off your signal, for example with aeroplane mode.
  3. Select Write the note.
  4. Type what you did and how the person seemed, then select Send the note.
  5. The note stays on the phone. Go back to Your visits to see it waiting.
  6. Turn your signal back on and select Send now.

Add to a note you sent

  1. On a visit you have written up, select Add to your note.
  2. Type your addition and select Send the addition. Your first note is never changed.
  3. When you finish, select Sign out. It clears the app from the phone.
Start as a carer

Safeguarding lead

AI has read each note and quoted the words behind every finding. You decide.

Review the day's notes in order

  1. Select Sign in as the safeguarding lead.
  2. The Review register lists the notes in the order to review them. Notes AI has not checked come first, then act today, act this visit, random checks and possible tampering.
  3. Select Open the first note to decide.

Decide each finding in a flagged note

  1. Read the note. The highlighted words are the exact quotes the findings rest on.
  2. For each finding, select Confirmed, Wrong category or Not a concern.
  3. If you chose Wrong category, choose the right one from the list below it.
  4. Select Save and open the next note.

Record your read of a note with no finding

  1. Some notes are random checks: AI found nothing in them, and a person reads them anyway.
  2. Under Anything the AI missed, choose I read the whole note: nothing missed, or the concern it missed.
  3. Select Save and open the next note. It will not save until you record your read.
Start as the safeguarding lead

Manager

See who might benefit from a conversation

  1. Select Sign in as the manager.
  2. Read the list, most likely to leave first. The top tenth are marked Suggested.
  3. Select What raises it most under a carer to see why.
  4. The list supports a conversation. It never decides anything about a person.
Start as the manager

Coordinator

Check the rota and a carer's route

  1. Select Sign in as the coordinator.
  2. The Rota shows who works on the day and the visits to arrange by hand, grouped by reason.
  3. Select a carer under Carers working to see their route and visits.

See the re-plan when a carer drops out

  1. On the rota, select See the re-plan.
  2. Read who takes each visit, which times move and who gets more work.

See what a visit import held back

  1. Above the rota, select Visit import.
  2. Rows under Quarantine were held back, each with its reasons.
  3. Select The row as the file had it to see a row exactly as it came.

Confirm a new client's referral

  1. Select Referrals, then a referral marked To confirm.
  2. Check each ticked box against its highlighted quote. Untick anything the referral does not say.
  3. An option marked Not stated: ask the referrer is a question, not a no.
  4. Tick I have asked the referrer, then select Confirm this profile.
  5. Who may go now lists each visit's rules and the carers who may go.
Start as the coordinator

About the AI

What it does

  • AI reads each carer's note for safeguarding concerns, signs that someone's health is getting worse, and missed tasks. It also reads each new referral into a draft profile.
  • Every finding and claim quotes the words it rests on, and the app checks that those words are really there. A person decides each one. AI never decides anything alone.
  • The AI is Gemma 4 26B A4B, an open model from Google. It runs on a separate computer, never in this online service.
  • In this demo, notes you write are not read by AI. The safeguarding lead sees them as Not checked by AI yet.

How well it did

  • On 177 made-up notes it had not seen before, it found 59 of the 60 that needed action the same day. Of the 86 notes it marked that way, 59 were right, so a person checks every one.
  • On made-up referrals, it found each item in at least 95% of the referrals that state it, except two visits: lunch (86 of 91) and tea (42 of 47).

Its limits, and the checks around it

  • It was tested only on made-up writing. A quote shows that the words are there, not that they mean the concern.
  • A random 7% of notes with no finding go to the lead too, so a person sees what AI misses.
  • A separate small checker looks for notes that seem to give the software instructions. It can only add a note to review, never remove one.
  • The manager's list comes from a simple statistical model, not the AI above. It learned from a made-up workforce built from Skills for Care's 2026 figures, and the effects of the rota are assumed. It supports a conversation and never decides anything about a person.

Good to know

  • The demo is set at 12:30 on Sunday 8 November 2026. The carer's day follows that time.
  • On a phone, you can add the carer app to your home screen from the browser's menu.
  • Every person, client and note is synthetic.
